    Frequently asked questions

    What is Memeception?

    An endless meme feed. Pick a category, then scroll (or press J) for the next meme. Every meme you see takes you one level deeper; the Depth counter keeps score.

    Where do the memes come from?

    From meme-api.com, which pulls public posts from Reddit. Each category maps to one or more subreddits. If a subreddit is closed, the category falls back to a similar one.

    How do I save a meme?

    Double-tap the image, tap the bookmark, or press S. Open the bookmark at the top to see them. They're stored in this browser's local storage, so use Export to keep a copy.

    Can I change the theme?

    Yes. It follows your device by default. Use the moon/sun button or the setting above to choose.
