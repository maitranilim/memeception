Frequently Asked Questions

What is Memeception?
A simple way to browse memes. Pick a category and tap Gimme Meme to load another one.
Where do the memes come from?
The site gets memes from meme-api.com. Categories point to different subreddits. Sometimes a request or image may fail to load.
How do I save a meme?
Tap the save button next to Gimme Meme. Open the bookmark button at the top to see saved memes. They're kept in this browser's local storage, so clearing site data removes them.
Can I change the theme?
Yes. Use the button at the top to switch between light and dark mode.
